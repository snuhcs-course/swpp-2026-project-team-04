## c. What AI did well
- Every rule change was replayed over all recordings before it went in; for example, the 3-frame top fold was
  dropped because it lost two knee reps.
- Setup frames showed 492 of 506 "Get into a push-up" failures came from the torso line alone, which matched the
  user's report (setup line 1.0 → 1.5).
- On the evaluation set, a per-rep replay split the 34 missed normal reps by cause (depth lines in shoulder widths,
  a one-frame knee latch, the bow line, the arm average) and took M1 from 46.0% to 79.4% with M2 kept at 90.8%.

## d. Hallucinations / errors
- Wrote set 8 of the recording standard as a straight-arm dip on the toes; the judge needs the knees down for
  `armsStraight`. Caught while answering the PM; the set was changed before recording.
- Wrote set 4 as coming up to "about 140°"; at that height the judge sees the top. Caught when the user said
  bent-arm reps counted.
- Blamed one tester's failures on a wide hand position; her normal sets had ordinary hand widths.
- Recommended option D (knee latch off) from the evaluation set alone; replaying the earlier fixtures, at the user's
  question, showed 10 cheat reps would count. Withdrawn.
- Wrote that the kneel check had rejected two head-nod reps; the reviewer's on/off replay showed the lockout check
  lets them count. The doc and test reasons were corrected before commit.

## g. Takeaway for Iteration 4
- Check a proposed change on data it was not tuned on (earlier fixtures or new recordings) before recommending it,
  and write recording instructions only after replaying the motion through the judge.
