GymRats characters (w3_rig.json)

Origin: own work of SWPP 2026 team 04. The six characters (GYM마우스, 그루, 곰탱이,
펭뚱이, 물렁범, RepBot) were designed for this app by Woohyun Song on 2026-11-13.
No third-party art, fonts or models are used, so no third-party licence applies.

Use in the app: the rig is not bundled as an asset. lib/models/character_rig_data.dart
holds the same JSON as a Dart string (test/models/character_rig_test.dart checks that
they match) and lib/widgets/character_figure.dart (CharacterFigure) draws it. Change
this file first, then copy it into that Dart string.
